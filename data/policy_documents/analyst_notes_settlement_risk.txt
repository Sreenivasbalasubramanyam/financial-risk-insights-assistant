ANALYST NOTES — SETTLEMENT RISK (SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL)
Document ID: AN-2024-SETTLEMENT-07
Author: Settlement Risk Analytics Team
Distribution: Internal — Risk and Treasury Operations

1. OVERVIEW
Settlement risk is the risk that a counterparty fails to deliver its side of a transaction
(cash or securities) after the Bank has already delivered its side, or that settlement occurs
outside of expected timing, exposing the Bank to principal or replacement-cost risk.

2. KEY OBSERVATIONS THIS QUARTER
Settlement fails increased 12% quarter-over-quarter, concentrated in cross-currency swaps and
FX forwards settling through correspondent banks in Asia-Pacific time zones. The most common
root cause (61% of fails) was nostro account funding timing mismatches rather than counterparty
credit issues. A smaller but notable share (18%) involved incorrect standard settlement
instructions (SSIs) that had not been updated after a counterparty changed custodians.

3. PVP AND DVP COVERAGE
Approximately 74% of FX settlement volume is currently covered by Payment-versus-Payment (PvP)
arrangements via CLS Bank, which eliminates principal settlement risk for those trades. The
remaining 26%, largely emerging-market currency pairs not eligible for CLS, settle on a
non-PvP basis and carry full principal risk during the settlement window.

4. RECOMMENDATIONS
(a) Expand SSI verification automation to flag stale custodian records older than 180 days.
(b) Negotiate PvP-eligible settlement for the top 5 emerging-market currency corridors by volume.
(c) Increase nostro pre-funding buffers for APAC cut-off windows by 20% during quarter-end
volume spikes. (d) Introduce a real-time settlement fail dashboard for the Treasury desk with
automatic escalation if a fail is not resolved within 4 hours.

5. LINKAGE TO COUNTERPARTY EXPOSURE POLICY
Repeated settlement fails with a single counterparty (three or more in a rolling 90-day period)
should be factored into the counterparty's internal risk rating and considered during the
quarterly exposure limit review described in the Counterparty Exposure Risk Policy.

6. NEXT REVIEW
This analysis will be refreshed next quarter with updated fail-rate statistics and CLS coverage
percentages following onboarding of two additional currency corridors.
