FRAUD DETECTION AND PREVENTION POLICY
Document ID: FD-2024-004 (Synthetic/Fictional — for demonstration purposes only)
Effective Date: March 1, 2024
Owner: Financial Crimes and Fraud Prevention Unit

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the standards Meridian Fictional Bank uses to detect, investigate, and
respond to suspected fraudulent activity across retail, commercial, and card payment channels.

2. FRAUD RISK SCORING
Every transaction is scored in real time by the Fraud Detection Engine (FDE) on a scale of
0-100, combining device fingerprinting, behavioral biometrics, velocity checks, and geolocation
anomalies. Transactions scoring above 85 are auto-declined pending manual review. Transactions
scoring between 60 and 85 are routed to the Tier 1 Fraud Analyst queue for review within
15 minutes during business hours and 60 minutes overnight.

3. VELOCITY AND PATTERN RULES
Automated rules flag: (a) more than 5 card-not-present transactions within a 10-minute window,
(b) a new payee added followed by a transfer exceeding $5,000 within 24 hours, (c) login from
a device or IP geolocation more than 500 miles from the customer's registered address followed
by a high-value transaction, and (d) structuring patterns consistent with multiple transactions
just below the $10,000 reporting threshold.

4. CASE INVESTIGATION WORKFLOW
Flagged cases are assigned a case ID and investigated by a Tier 1 analyst within the SLA above.
Confirmed fraud cases are escalated to Tier 2 for chargeback processing, customer notification,
and card reissuance. Suspected first-party fraud or organized fraud rings are escalated to the
Special Investigations Unit (SIU).

5. CUSTOMER LIABILITY AND REIMBURSEMENT
Verified unauthorized transactions are reimbursed to the customer within 10 business days per
Regulation E requirements, subject to completion of the required affidavit of fraud.

6. MODEL GOVERNANCE
The FDE scoring model is retrained quarterly using labeled fraud outcomes and validated by the
Model Risk Management team before deployment. Model drift is monitored weekly using population
stability index (PSI); a PSI above 0.25 triggers mandatory model review.

7. REPORTING
Confirmed fraud losses exceeding $25,000 in a single incident, or patterns suggesting organized
fraud rings, must be reported to the Financial Crimes Committee within 48 hours and may trigger
a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) filing per AML policy.

8. REVIEW CYCLE
This policy is reviewed semi-annually by the Fraud Prevention Unit and approved by the Chief
Risk Officer.
