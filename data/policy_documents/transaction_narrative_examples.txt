TRANSACTION NARRATIVE EXAMPLES (SYNTHETIC / FICTIONAL)
Document ID: TN-2024-SAMPLES
Purpose: Illustrative transaction narratives used for analyst training and RAG demonstration.
All customer names, account numbers, and amounts below are fictional.

NARRATIVE 1 — Case #TXN-88231
Customer "J. Alvarez" (fictional) initiated four wire transfers of $9,400, $9,600, $9,200, and
$9,800 to four different beneficiaries over a 48-hour period, each just under the $10,000
Currency Transaction Report threshold. Pattern is consistent with structuring behavior described
in the AML Compliance Policy. Analyst flagged for SAR consideration; account placed under
enhanced monitoring for 90 days.

NARRATIVE 2 — Case #TXN-90112
A commercial account associated with "Blue Harbor Logistics" (fictional shell entity) received
three incoming wires totaling $480,000 from an offshore jurisdiction on the FATF grey list,
followed within 24 hours by an outbound wire of $475,000 to an unrelated third party with no
apparent business purpose. Rapid pass-through movement of funds is a classic layering indicator
under the AML Compliance Policy; case escalated to the Financial Crimes Committee.

NARRATIVE 3 — Case #TXN-77490
Retail customer reported a $2,340 card-not-present purchase at an electronics retailer they did
not recognize. Fraud Detection Engine score was 91 at time of transaction; device fingerprint did
not match any of the customer's previously seen devices, and billing geolocation was 1,200 miles
from the customer's registered address. Consistent with Fraud Detection Policy velocity rule (c).
Transaction was auto-declined; customer confirmed no authorization; case closed as prevented fraud.

NARRATIVE 4 — Case #TXN-81005
Counterparty "Silverline Capital Partners" (fictional, non-investment-grade rated) breached 92%
of its assigned exposure limit following a large derivative mark-to-market swing after a sector-wide
credit spread widening event. Amber alert triggered per Counterparty Exposure Risk Policy Section 5;
Counterparty Risk Committee reviewed the position and reduced the limit allocation by 15% pending
a scheduled ratings review.

NARRATIVE 5 — Case #TXN-93217
Settlement of a cross-currency swap with counterparty "Northgate Trading" failed to settle on
value date due to a nostro account funding shortfall. Settlement risk was elevated for approximately
6 hours before funds were located and the trade settled T+1. Incident logged per Analyst Notes on
Settlement Risk; no financial loss realized, but operational root cause review was opened.
