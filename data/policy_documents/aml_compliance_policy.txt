ANTI-MONEY LAUNDERING (AML) COMPLIANCE POLICY
Document ID: AML-2024-002 (Synthetic/Fictional — for demonstration purposes only)
Effective Date: February 1, 2024
Owner: BSA/AML Compliance Office

1. PURPOSE
This policy sets the standards for detecting, deterring, and reporting money laundering and
terrorist financing activity in accordance with fictional analogues of the Bank Secrecy Act
and FinCEN reporting requirements, for the purposes of this internal demonstration.

2. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD)
All new customers undergo risk-based CDD at onboarding, including identity verification,
beneficial ownership identification for entities (25% or greater ownership threshold), and
screening against sanctions and politically exposed persons (PEP) lists. High-risk customers,
including money services businesses, cash-intensive businesses, and customers in high-risk
jurisdictions, undergo Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) with review at least every 12 months.

3. STRUCTURING DETECTION
Structuring — breaking large cash or wire transactions into smaller amounts to evade the
$10,000 Currency Transaction Report (CTR) threshold — is a designated red flag. Automated
surveillance flags any customer with three or more transactions between $8,000 and $9,999
within a rolling 7-day window for mandatory analyst review.

4. SUSPICIOUS ACTIVITY REPORTING (SAR)
Analysts must document the basis for any SAR decision within the case management system.
SARs must be filed within 30 calendar days of initial detection of suspicious activity (or
60 days if additional time is needed to identify a suspect). Common triggers include
structuring, rapid layering of funds through shell entities, transactions with no apparent
business or lawful purpose, and unexplained wires to or from FATF grey-list jurisdictions.

5. LAYERING AND SHELL ENTITY INDICATORS
Indicators include: pass-through accounts with minimal balance retention, incoming funds from
one jurisdiction immediately wired to an unrelated third party, shared registered addresses
across multiple corporate customers, and inconsistency between stated business purpose and
observed transaction activity.

6. TRAINING AND GOVERNANCE
All customer-facing and risk staff complete AML training annually. The BSA Officer reports
program effectiveness, SAR volume, and emerging typologies to the Board Audit Committee
quarterly.

7. RECORD RETENTION
CDD records, SAR filings, and supporting case files are retained for a minimum of 5 years
from the date of filing or account closure, whichever is later.

8. REVIEW CYCLE
This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to reflect changes in regulatory
guidance and emerging typologies identified by the Financial Crimes Committee.
